01 / About

A conductor shaped by
the orchestra.

Nathan Budiman is an Indonesian conductor working across orchestral, chamber, choral, and historically informed repertoire.

He is Music Director of the Bandung Symphony Orchestra and Music Director of the Jakarta Baroque Collective, and serves as conductor of Euphonia Camerata. He has also led ensembles including the Parahyangan Orchestra, PLUS Choir UPH, GRII Karawaci Ensemble, and Pratama Voice.

Nathan is currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in music composition at Universitas Pelita Harapan. His conducting studies include conducting practicum with Budi Utomo Prabowo, while his broader musical practice is informed by active orchestral experience as a violist, violinist, pianist, and continuo player.

He has performed and conducted at venues including Aula Simfonia Jakarta, the Arntz-Geise Auditorium of UNPAR, and the Cathedral of Saint Peter in Bandung, and has collaborated with musicians across orchestral, chamber, choral, and early-music settings.

Conducting in performance

Conducting philosophy

“The conductor is shaped by the orchestra as much as the orchestra is shaped by the conductor.”

Nathan approaches conducting as a collaborative practice: listening to the orchestra, responding to its musicians, and shaping a shared interpretation rather than simply directing from above.

Rehearsal · orchestral work

Conducting practice

Listening, rehearsal, gesture, and collaboration.

Nathan's conducting practice spans orchestral, chamber, and choral work, with particular interest in the relationship between score, rehearsal process, and the musicians in front of him.

His work also extends into historically informed performance, where practical experience in continuo, harpsichord, organ, and chamber music informs his approach to articulation, balance, phrasing, and ensemble communication.

Historically informed performance

Baroque repertoire, continuo, and keyboard practice.

Nathan's historically informed performance practice is rooted in playing as a continuo keyboardist and harpsichordist, including work with the Bandung Baroque Trio and the Jakarta Baroque Collective.

His experience includes harpsichord, organ/continuo, piano accompaniment, and partimento improvisation, with repertoire by Bach, Biber, Lully, and other Baroque composers.
